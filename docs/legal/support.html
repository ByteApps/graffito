What Graffito does

Graffito writes short, encrypted notes onto the Bitcoin blockchain, signed by a key that never leaves your device, and reads them back on any device from that key alone. Notes can be private to you or directed to another Bitcoin address so only its holder can read them.

Getting started

On first launch you create a new key in-app (a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase) or import an existing one (recovery words, xprv, WIF, hex, or a SeedQR). Write your recovery phrase down and keep it safe — it is the only way to recover your notes and funds if you lose the device. Your key is stored in the device keychain and revealed only after you authenticate.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Graffito reads from and broadcasts to the Bitcoin network, so it needs a connection to sync balances, fetch notes, and send transactions.

Can I try it without spending real bitcoin?

Yes. In Settings you can switch the network to testnet4 (or signet / regtest) to exercise every flow — compose, sign, broadcast, read-back — using coins that have no monetary value.

I got a new phone. How do I recover my notes?

Install Graffito and import your recovery phrase. Every notebook rebuilds itself from the chain — your notes and balances come back from your key alone.

Are my private notes really private?

The contents of private and directed notes are encrypted so only you or your chosen recipient can read them. The transaction itself is public and permanent on the blockchain. See the Privacy Policy.

Is my data collected?

No. The app has no accounts, ads, or tracking, and no server that receives your data. See the Privacy Policy.

Graffito is a self-custody Bitcoin app. Your keys, your notes, your device. No account required.
