Privacy Policy — Graffito

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Graffito does not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We operate no servers that receive your data.

Your keys stay on your device

Graffito is a self-custody app. The key that signs and encrypts your notes is created or imported on your device and stored only in the platform keychain (Apple Keychain on iOS and Mac, the Android Keystore on Android). It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and revealing it always requires you to authenticate. If you enable your device's encrypted keychain backup (for example iCloud Keychain), your key is backed up by that Apple service under your control — we never see it.

Network use

Graffito reads and writes to the public Bitcoin blockchain. To do so it connects to public Bitcoin block-explorer / mempool APIs to fetch balances and transactions and to broadcast the transactions you sign. Those third-party servers can see your device's IP address and the addresses and transactions you look up or broadcast — this is inherent to using any public blockchain, and is true of every Bitcoin wallet. Graffito sends no name, email, account, or other personal identifier with these requests. You can point the app at a Bitcoin node of your choice in Settings.

Notes are written to a public, permanent ledger

Notes you anchor are recorded on the Bitcoin blockchain, which is public and permanent by design. Public notes can be read by anyone. Private and directed notes have their contents encrypted so only you or your chosen recipient can read them, but the fact that a transaction exists — and its on-chain metadata — is public and cannot be deleted. This is a property of Bitcoin itself, not something Graffito controls. Only write what you are comfortable committing permanently.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
