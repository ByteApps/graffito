Graffito turns the Bitcoin blockchain into your personal, tamper-evident notebook.

Compose a short note, encrypt it, sign it with a key that never leaves your device, and anchor it on-chain. Later — on any device — read your notes straight back from the chain. Keep a note private to yourself, or send it to one or more Bitcoin addresses so only the holders can read it.

- Your keys, your device. Create a new 12/24-word key in-app or import an existing one (words, xprv, WIF, hex, or SeedQR). Key material lives only in the device keychain and is revealed only after you authenticate.
- Notebooks. Keep as many notebooks as you like, each its own address derived from the same key — one for gifts, one for a project, one to archive when it's done.
- Private by design. Bodies are encrypted; only you (or your chosen recipients) can read them. No account, no sign-up, no tracking server.
- Send to people. Direct a note to any taproot address — or several at once — and optionally tuck in a gift of sats. Reply and reply-all keep the thread going.
- Filter unwanted senders. Anyone can send you a note, so you decide who shows up — mute a sender and their notes drop out of your view.
- Contacts on every device. Save the people you write to as named contacts; your address book syncs privately over iCloud, so it's there on your iPhone and your Mac alike.
- Read it anywhere. Your notes live on the most durable ledger there is — recover them on a new device from your key alone.
- Full control. Choose fees, pick which coins you spend, sweep or consolidate your wallet, and manage your addresses. Import a watch-only key to read and spend with an external signer.

Bitcoin knowledge helps, but Graffito handles the cryptography for you.
