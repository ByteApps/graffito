• Syncing fetches only what is new instead of walking a notebook's
  whole history, on every backend (explorer, Bitcoin Core, Electrum).
• Notes appear as they arrive: the first page renders right away and
  the rest streams in behind it.
• The notes list scales to thousands of notes: it renders a window and
  grows as you scroll; the header shows the total.
