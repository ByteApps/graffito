• Syncing a notebook now fetches only what is new instead of walking its
  whole history every time, on every backend (mempool-style explorers,
  your own Bitcoin Core node, and Electrum servers).
• Notes appear as they arrive: the first page renders right away and the
  rest streams in behind it. Long histories no longer stall the screen.
• The notes list scales to thousands of notes — it renders a window and
  grows as you scroll; the header shows the total.
• Quiet background checks on Core and Electrum are now a couple of small
  requests instead of re-downloading every transaction.
